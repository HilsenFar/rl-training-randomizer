## Training pack data

Training packs are created and shared by the Rocket League community. Pack codes
and metadata in the built-in collections come from public sources, credited in
each collection's `source` field:

- **`reddit-lander1984.json`** — Lander1984's rated master list, posted on
  r/RocketLeagueSchool.
- **Prejump** (via `bin/scrape.mjs`, when you run it) — the public training-pack
  database at <https://prejump.com/training-packs>. Codes and metadata are
  Prejump's / the creators' own; this tool only collects and rolls them.

Codes belong to the packs' creators. This project claims no ownership of any
pack.
